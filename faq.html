Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your home begins to shine.

How does the quote process work?

Complete our form with your contact details, preferred style, and property photos. We will review everything and send a personalized proposal.

Is the quote free?

Yes. Your initial personalized quote is completely free and there is no obligation to book.

Do I need to provide photos?

Photos are optional, but clear images of the roofline, trees, and landscape help us prepare a more accurate design and estimate.

What areas can you decorate?

We can decorate rooflines, windows, doors, trees, landscaping, wreaths, and custom residential or commercial displays.

What light colors are available?

Popular choices include warm white, cool white, and multicolor. If you are unsure, we can recommend the best option for your property.

How early should I reserve installation?

Holiday dates fill quickly, so we recommend requesting your quote and reserving your installation as early as possible.

Do you serve businesses as well as homes?

Yes. We create custom Christmas displays for both residential and commercial properties.

Does the quote include removal?

Yes, removal is included in the quote.

Does the quote include storage of the lights?

The quote does not include storage of the light but we offer the storage service as an extra charge.
